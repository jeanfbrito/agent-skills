The trail is the report

2026-10-09

Write as you work. Each step of a cycle writes one note to the trail at the moment it happens: the pick, the prediction, each attempt, each capture and measure, keep or revert, the commit and the end. Notes are not saved for the end.

A kill. A later run reads the trail and continues the open cycle from its last note, so a kill loses at most the step in progress.

Lock. Each note renews the lock. Before a step that can take more than an hour, write its try note first.

The agent. Starts empty and reads its state from the trail, so no cycle carries the history of the ones before it.

Report. Each note also rebuilds the HTML report. The trail is the report: it organizes the screenshots and metrics so anyone can see where the work is. Nobody builds it as a separate job.

Next run. If the brief says IN PROGRESS, continue that cycle from its last note. Do not repeat a step that has a note, and do not retry what the brief lists under DO NOT RETRY.

End. The end note carries the status change, the progress on the metric and the next step. Then unlock, print one status line, and stop.

One idea. Marks welcome. Frame: score.
